# Inventory

Inventory shows your signed-in Steam backpack. It belongs to your Steam account, not to a profile: favorites, saved searches, layouts and history follow the account.

It loads when you open the pane, after TF2 closes, and when you press **Refresh**. Each read briefly shows you as in TF2 on Steam, so execs never reads it on a timer. TF2 must be closed.

## Arranging

Drag items, select several with Ctrl or Shift, or use the sort menu. Every change is a draft until you **Review changes** and apply it; **Undo** and **Redo** work on the draft. Clear search filters before dragging. Favorite or protected items never move.

Restoring a saved layout only changes the draft. New and protected items keep their place.

## Crafting and deleting

Both change your Steam backpack permanently, and neither can be undone.

- **Craft** combines, smelts or crafts a random hat from plain metal, one recipe at a time, and shows exactly which items it uses. Random hats need a premium TF2 account; Steam picks the hat.
- **Delete** removes one item after a confirmation.

If Steam's answer is unclear, execs keeps the operation pending and asks you to **Reconcile**, which reads your backpack again instead of repeating it.

**History** lists operations made from this PC; it isn't Steam's transaction history.

## Item art

Item pictures come from Steam's public inventory images for your account, so painted weapons and war paints look the way TF2 shows them. If they can't load, execs uses art from your TF2 files.
