# App settings

## Appearance

**Follow system** respects your device's reduced-motion setting. **Reduce** turns off execs' transitions even if your device allows them.

## Updates

execs checks for updates on startup (you can turn that off) and installs one only when you click. Updates come from execs' GitHub releases.

## Storage

- **Profiles**: your saved profiles and their files.
- **Restore points**: kept until you delete them or newer ones replace them.
- **Downloads**: HUD catalog, comfig.app sounds and Casual setup files. **Clear downloads** removes them; they download again when needed.
- **Retired downloads**: left over from features execs no longer has. Safe to clear.
- **Logs**: crash logs and recent activity, for bug reports.
- **Recovery and sources**: recovery data, original game files execs needs to undo changes, sounds you added and HUD backups. These are never cleared.

**Tidy up again** removes leftovers from older versions of execs. Packs, your own files and hand edits are never touched.

## Health

Checks your TF2 install, the active cfg loader, interrupted changes, every profile's saved files, Steam Cloud's copy of `config.cfg` and what works offline. It changes nothing.

execs keeps Steam's local copy of `config.cfg` up to date; Steam uploads it when it syncs, which execs can't confirm.

## Support

**Copy diagnostics** copies a short report for a bug report. It includes your install path and active profile name, and never your file contents or passwords.

## Credits

The community projects execs builds on. **Read full notices** opens every credit and licence.

## Uninstall

Uninstalling execs leaves your TF2 setup as it is: cfg files, HUD and custom files stay in TF2.

If Casual setup changed TF2's own files, **Restore stock files** puts them back first. **Also delete execs data** removes profiles, restore points, downloads and settings; export any profiles you want to keep. It's available only after stock files are restored, because execs keeps the original game files in its data.
