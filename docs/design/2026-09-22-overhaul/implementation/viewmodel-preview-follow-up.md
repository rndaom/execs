# Viewmodels builder and preview release gate

The owner confirmed on September 24 that the Viewmodels builder remains in the approved 0.2.0 scope. The current development branch temporarily refuses builds after removing the CompVMInstaller animation ZIP, group table and screenshots. This is a release-blocking implementation gap, not approval to ship import-only Viewmodels. Previously built VPK bytes and recorded choices remain intact through switch and export/import.

## Replacement boundary

Build a new engine from the player's confirmed TF2 installation and independently derived item, activity, sequence and bone relationships. Do not copy the CompVMInstaller source archive, its group table or its preview JPEGs. The replacement must provide equivalent class/group choices, combined selections and Full/Weapon hide modes. A legacy `yttrium-1` choice is a compatibility input; match it to independently derived groups only after that graph is frozen. An unmatched choice requires explicit review while preserving its old VPK and record bytes.

The read-only [stock mapping study](../../../audits/2026-09-23-program-audit/d7-stock-mapping-study.md) parsed 862 local animations and 748 sequences across nine installed class models on patch 10828683. The [compatibility bridge](../../../audits/2026-09-23-program-audit/d7-viewmodel-local-rebuild-bridge.md) found exact names for all 405 distinct legacy sequence stems in those models. Those results establish addressable names on one patch, not complete item reachability or rendered behavior. The [Scout root](../../../audits/2026-09-23-program-audit/d7-scout-root-offset-probe.md) and [weapon-bone](../../../audits/2026-09-23-program-audit/d7-scout-weapon-bone-probe.md) probes are scratch byte experiments, not working builders.

The first replacement slice is the read-only [Rust stock animation index](../../../apps/desktop/src-tauri/core/src/viewmodel_source.rs). It binds to a validated app-440 root, bounds and checks the nine VPK members, parses the SDK sequence/activity/blend metadata, fingerprints every MDL, and rechecks the inputs before returning. Its [probe](../../../apps/desktop/src-tauri/core/examples/viewmodel_source_probe.rs) can inspect an installed copy without creating a pack. On the local patch 10828683 it found the same nine class models, 862 animations, 748 sequences and 859 blend references as the earlier independent Python audit. This is source evidence for the next item/activity mapping slice; Build stays disabled.

The bone-hierarchy follow-up indexes the same nine verified MDLs using Valve's `studiohdr_t` and `mstudiobone_t` layout. Its [read-only probe](../../../apps/desktop/src-tauri/core/examples/viewmodel_bones_probe.rs) found 549 bones on patch 10828683, including 152 `weapon_bone` or `vm_weapon_bone` names; all 152 are direct children of `bip_hand_L` or `bip_hand_R`. This is a structural prerequisite for a hands-visible Weapon mode, not a decoded pose, visual result or working transform.

The [pose-record inventory](../../../apps/desktop/src-tauri/core/src/viewmodel_pose.rs) parses local bone-record headers and animation sections against the verified MDL fingerprints without changing bytes. Its [installed probe](../../../apps/desktop/src-tauri/core/examples/viewmodel_pose_probe.rs) covered all nine class models and 862 animations on patch 10828683. Seven special delta animations need another source path, and eight weapon records have no following offset to bound their payload for a writer. The regular records include both raw and compressed position channels; their payloads and rendering remain undecoded. The probe accepts Valve's `bone=255` end marker. This inventory narrows the transform work but does not enable Build or a preview.

The second slice is the read-only [Rust item catalog and explicit replacement linker](../../../apps/desktop/src-tauri/core/src/viewmodel_items.rs). It resolves installed item-schema prefabs, keeps loadout slots separate from animation roles, records common and per-team activity replacements, fingerprints and rechecks the schema, then looks up each replacement target in the class MDL without dropping unmatched targets or blend references. Its first [probe](../../../apps/desktop/src-tauri/core/examples/viewmodel_items_probe.rs) read 10,391 class-eligible item definitions on patch 10828683 and counted 1,044 raw visual-block replacement candidates, 770 with a direct local MDL sequence. The Shortstop and Wrangler role/slot pairs agree with the independent audit. Valve's [team visual selection](https://github.com/ValveSoftware/source-sdk-2013/blob/b8cfb12c0e083a2ef5b2f9f9b50f3902fa034474/src/game/shared/econ/econ_item_schema.h) and [visual block inheritance](https://github.com/ValveSoftware/source-sdk-2013/blob/b8cfb12c0e083a2ef5b2f9f9b50f3902fa034474/src/game/shared/econ/econ_item_schema.cpp) require selecting an effective RED or BLU block rather than treating the neutral and team blocks as additive. The corrected read-only probe reports 2,068 effective item/class/team/activity candidates, 1,520 with direct local MDL sequences. A candidate repeated for both teams counts twice. These counts are **not** item reachability or builder coverage: weapon-script roles, activity translation, inspect routing, specialized runtime paths and retail behavior remain to be combined and verified. Build remains disabled.

The third slice is the read-only [stock weapon-script role index](../../../apps/desktop/src-tauri/core/src/viewmodel_scripts.rs). It bounds and CRC-checks the installed `tf_weapon_*.ctx`/`.txt` entries, decodes their scripts, fingerprints and rechecks the bytes, and records `WeaponType` without guessing a match for a renamed `item_class`. Valve's item-schema parser maps `FORCE_NOT_USED` to a negative override, so those items take their role from the weapon script; `PASSTIME_BALL` is a recognized item role. Installed patch 10828683 yielded 78 scripts and 496 class-eligible `tf_weapon_*` items: 106 recognized item overrides, 372 exact script roles, and 18 missing exact script matches. All 18 exact misses share `tf_weapon_shotgun`; the installed archive has multiple class-specific shotgun script stems, so a source-backed class-to-script resolver is still needed. The [probe](../../../apps/desktop/src-tauri/core/examples/viewmodel_scripts_probe.rs) does not establish that an exact script path is the runtime source for every item. Build remains disabled.

The class-aware follow-up maps the generic shotgun item's Engineer, Soldier, Heavy and Pyro cases to **candidates** with the corresponding installed script stems. This is based on Valve's [separate weapon registrations](https://github.com/ValveSoftware/source-sdk-2013/blob/b8cfb12c0e083a2ef5b2f9f9b50f3902fa034474/src/game/shared/tf/tf_weapon_shotgun.cpp#L24-L35), the installed single-class shotgun definitions, and their script files. On patch 10828683, the class-aware probe reports 55 candidate item/class pairs: Engineer 13 and Soldier, Heavy and Pyro 14 each. Multi-class descendants remain candidates until the retail equip alias is verified. The resolver reports them separately from exact script matches; this does not close the reachability or builder gate.

The pure [hand-activity translator](../../../apps/desktop/src-tauri/core/src/viewmodel_activity.rs) implements the functional role/action families of Valve's [pinned first-person activity table](https://github.com/ValveSoftware/source-sdk-2013/blob/b8cfb12c0e083a2ef5b2f9f9b50f3902fa034474/src/game/shared/tf/tf_weaponbase.cpp), including the distinct `PRIMARY2` reload targets and identity entries for special melee actions. It reports whether a role rule matched, so an unchanged fallback does not masquerade as a confirmed mapping. It has no disk or game side effects. Item/team replacement precedence, inspect's loadout path, specialized weapon behavior and retail reachability still need the combined graph and in-game verification.

The class-slot follow-up also retains each class's effective loadout slot from `used_by_classes`, separately from the default `item_slot` and animation role. The installed patch has 37 item/class slots that differ from the item default, including shotgun variants. TF2 treats the presence of a class key as usable and a value of `1` as the default slot; the [pinned item-schema parser](https://github.com/ValveSoftware/source-sdk-2013/blob/b8cfb12c0e083a2ef5b2f9f9b50f3902fa034474/src/game/shared/tf/tf_item_schema.cpp) provides that rule. This is still static schema metadata, not proof of an equip or animation path.

The attachment-routing follow-up keeps the schema's `attach_to_hands` and `attach_to_hands_vm_only` flags distinct. Valve's [weapon viewmodel selection](https://github.com/ValveSoftware/source-sdk-2013/blob/b8cfb12c0e083a2ef5b2f9f9b50f3902fa034474/src/game/shared/tf/tf_weaponbase.cpp) uses `ShouldAttachToHands()` before choosing the shared class hands model. Of 496 installed `tf_weapon_*` definitions on patch 10828683, 487 request that shared model and one sets the separate viewmodel-only attachment flag. This narrows source candidates; it does not establish in-game item availability or animation reachability.

## Acceptance before enabling Build

1. Bind every read to the confirmed app-440 root and bounded, CRC-checked installed sources. Reject missing, malformed, mixed-version or updated inputs; never write to the TF2 archive while deriving or compiling.
2. Derive class/group membership independently from installed item schema, weapon scripts, activity replacements and all MDL blend references. Cover unusual item behavior and all nine classes. Compare the frozen result to saved legacy choices without silently changing their meaning.
3. Build only in bounded scratch space, validate the resulting models and VPK, then install with the existing write gate and recoverable profile transaction. Full and Weapon modes must work for single and combined selections on Windows and Linux.
4. Exercise every derived group in retail TF2 with a reaching item, plus representative alternate items and unaffected actions. Check draw, idle, fire, reload, inspect, melee, transitions, both teams, third-person effects where applicable, Casual and stock restoration. A parser or compiler pass alone does not satisfy this gate.
5. Preserve old compiled and imported packs through reopen, switch and export/import. A new stock-derived record needs source fingerprints and a deliberate export/import design. [Valve's TF2 mod guidance](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/sdk/uploading/distributing_source_engine) says a mod may load TF2 content from a local install but may not redistribute TF2 game files; the [export review](../../../audits/2026-09-23-program-audit/d7-valve-export-rights.md) records why a generated VPK cannot simply be assumed safe to share in a profile ZIP.
6. Show only previews captured from the verified built model and the player's installed TF2 assets. Capture and check a genuine hands-visible image per supported class/group, compare representative images with retail rendering, and key caches to input model and build revision. Until then, label imagery as illustrative or omit the rendered claim.

Valve's [Source model viewer documentation](https://developer.valvesoftware.com/wiki/Half-Life_Model_Viewer_%28Source%29) describes a screenshot option but also notes reliability and game-directory constraints. Establish a confined viewer write boundary before using it as the preview pipeline; editing a concept image cannot verify the built result.
